# PayScaleBD Support

For help, corrections, source questions, or accessibility feedback, email [ventures.coelia@gmail.com](mailto:ventures.coelia@gmail.com).

When reporting a calculation issue, include the selected grade, basic salary, housing category, eligible-child count, age group, GPF rate, and salary stage. Do not include your name, employee ID, department, or other personal information.

PayScaleBD is an independent, unofficial calculator and cannot issue or change an official pay fixation. Confirm entitlement questions with your accounts office or the appropriate government authority.

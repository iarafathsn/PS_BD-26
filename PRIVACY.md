# PayScaleBD Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

PayScaleBD is developed and published by Coelia Ventures. It is an offline salary-estimation app that does not require an account. The app itself does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Information handled on the device

- Salary selections and calculated results remain in the app while you use it and are not transmitted or saved by PayScaleBD.
- Language and appearance preferences are saved locally on your device using Apple-provided preferences storage.
- PDF salary statements are generated locally. If you choose to share or save a PDF, the destination you select is governed by that destination's privacy practices.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

This version of PayScaleBD contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

## Data retention and deletion

PayScaleBD does not operate a server and retains no user data. You can remove locally saved preferences by deleting the app.

## Support communications

If you contact support, Coelia Ventures receives the information you voluntarily provide and uses it only to respond to your request.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [ventures.coelia@gmail.com](mailto:ventures.coelia@gmail.com).

This policy may be updated when the app's privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

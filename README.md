# Pay Scale BD Application Resources

This repository hosts public policy, support, and release-related resources for the PayScaleBD iOS app.

PayScaleBD is developed and published by **Coelia Ventures**. It is an independent, unofficial salary-estimation app and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by the Government of Bangladesh.

Resources are maintained on version-specific production branches. The `production/1.0` branch contains the policies and support information applicable to PayScaleBD version 1.0.

Policies may be updated when the app’s functionality, data practices, support information, or legal requirements change.

## Disclaimer

PayScaleBD provides unofficial estimates for informational purposes. It does not issue official salary fixation orders, payslips, or entitlement decisions. Users should confirm calculations and eligibility with their accounts office or the appropriate government authority.
